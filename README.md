# Uploaded-Liquid-Glass-Watermark
Upload Liqiuid Glass Watermark for Videos, Photos, Live Photos, and more – it's all free!




---

⚙️ Function

- ✅ Supports watermarks for videos, photos, and Live Photos.
- ✅ Custom Logo: Choose your preferred logo, including the preset "Leica" logo, which can be placed before or after the camera name.
- ✅ Custom Camera Name: Enter any camera name you like. If camera information is detected in the image metadata, the system will automatically use it. Otherwise, a button allows you to enter the name manually.
- ✅ Custom Fonts: Minecraft-style fonts are available to give your watermark a unique look.
